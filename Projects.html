Space shooter game

Space Shooter is a game where the player controls a spaceship that shoots enemies while dodging their attacks in order to survive. After the player defeats all the enemies in a level, the game advances to the next level and the same style of gameplay continues with new enemies and challenges.

Pollution Patrol

My game focuses on ocean pollution, especially the problem of unnecessary objects and waste being dumped into the water. These items can harm marine life and damage underwater ecosystems like coral reefs. In the game, the keys represent this pollution, showing how human actions can negatively impact the ocean if waste is not properly managed.
